# Writing questions for Top of the World

Thanks for helping! You don't need to know anything technical. The whole job is: open the
writer, write five questions, press **Publish**.

## Every time you want to write

1. Double-click **Write questions** in the project folder.
   (A black window opens — that's normal. Leave it open while you work.)
2. Your browser opens the question writer.
3. Write a day.
4. Press **Publish**.
5. Close the black window. Done.

You can't clash with anyone else, even if you're both writing at the same moment. Your day
simply joins the end of the queue.

## Writing a day

Each day is **five questions**, getting harder from Q1 to Q5. Every question asks the player to
click the country at **#1** on a ranked list.

For each one:

- **The question** reads as "Click the country **…**", so write the ending —
  *"with the most volcanoes"*, *"with the longest coastline"*.
- **Where you got it** — the page or source you used, e.g. *"List of countries by coastline –
  Wikipedia"*. Players see this afterwards.
- **The answers** — copy a ranked table straight from Wikipedia or a spreadsheet and paste it
  into the dashed box, then press Add. It works out the countries by itself. You need
  **10 to 20** answers.
  - A row in red means it couldn't work out the country. Pick one from the list or delete the row.
  - Tick **tie** on a row that shares the place above it.
  - Use ↑ ↓ to reorder, and ▲ ▼ to move a whole question to a different difficulty.

Before you write one, type a word into **Search past questions** at the top left to check it
hasn't been done.

**Play preview** plays your day exactly as a real player would see it. It doesn't affect your
real score or anybody else's.

## Saving vs publishing

- **Save** keeps your work on your own computer. Save as often as you like.
- **Publish** sends it to everyone and puts it in the queue. Only finished days get published —
  the writer tells you if something is missing.

A day is finished when all five questions have a question, a source, and 10–20 answers. The
sidebar says **Ready to publish** when you're there.

You don't choose which date your day lands on. It goes to the end of the queue, and the writer
shows you roughly when that'll be.

## If something looks wrong

Nothing you do here can break the game or lose anyone else's work, so don't worry.

- **A message in red** — read it; it usually says exactly what to do.
- **"Ask Adam"** — send him a screenshot. Your questions are safe on your computer.
- **Anything else odd** — close the black window, double-click **Write questions** again.

If Adam asks you to, open the black window and type this, then read him what it says:

```
npm run doctor
```
